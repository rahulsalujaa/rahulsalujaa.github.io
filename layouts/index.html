Hi, I'm Rahul Saluja

BNY · CFA L2 · INVESTING & PERSONAL FINANCE

I write about investing, personal finance, and data-driven insights into Indian markets.

Through my content, I try to make personal finance and investing feel less intimidating for everyday investors in India. Covering mutual funds, portfolio construction, risk-return trade-offs, and the wealth-building ideas that don't get talked about enough.

The part I genuinely enjoy is the analytical deep-dive on historical simulations, scenario analysis, Monte Carlo methods — pressure-testing what looks good on paper before it has to survive the real market.

With 4+ years of diverse finance experience and CFA Level 2 cleared, I also bring hands-on experience working with Python and multi-agentic AI systems, using AI as a research partner to handle execution, so I can focus on the thinking that actually matters.

My philosophy is straightforward: numbers over noise, frameworks over products, clarity over complexity. Most investors aren't short on choices, they're short on the lens to compare them.

Researching out loud. Sharing what I learn. One thoughtful post at a time.

Mutual Fund

For every ₹100 sitting in Indian bank accounts, ₹29 is now in mutual funds. Six years ago, it was just ₹16

For every ₹100 sitting in an Indian bank account, ₹28.5 is now in mutual funds. Six years ago, that number was just ₹16. That single shift tells you more about how India’s money mindset has changed than any headline ever could. But it doesn’t tell you where the money is going — and that’s where the story gets interesting. I pulled six years of AMFI data (FY20 to FY26) and broke down every rupee. The numbers paint a clear picture: India isn’t just investing more — it’s investing very differently than it used to. ...
